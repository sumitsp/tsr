Dear Provider Group,
This letter is to inform you OptumCare Arizona is now beginning its risk adjustment chart review process, as part of our commitment to quality patient care and provider support. As you know, risk adjustment is the Centers for Medicare and Medicaid Services (CMS) payment methodology to health plans. This methodology is dependent on accurate diagnosis coding. Reviewing medical chart documentation will enable OptumCare Arizona to identify conditions that may exist for plan members but may not have been coded or captured. Ensuring comprehensive care can be received for the member.
A representative from Optum will contact you shortly to provide you with retrieval options and a list of the requested members' medical records for services rendered from Jan. 1, 2025 to the present. Please be aware you may receive multiple requests due to availability of additional member data. Please provide all possible assistance with the medical record retrieval efforts. We ask that you treat the request for records with high priority. There are many options available to assist with the chart retrieval process:
Preferred Methods:
Secure Remote EMR Retrieval
Onsite Retrieval (An Optum technician will come onsite to transfer files)
Other Methods:
Secure Provider Portal (Upload directly to Optum, see instructions on chart request)
Fax or Mail (See instructions on chart request)
For each medical record, the following information is needed for services rendered from 01/01/2025 to present.
For Physicians   For Hospitals
Progress notes for face-to-face office visit   Progress notes
History and physical   Consultation reports
Consult notes   Radiology reports
Demographics sheet   Prescription for laboratory services
Problem list   History and physical exam / annual physical exam
Lab and screening results   Discharge summary
Signature log - CMS requirement   Emergency room records
